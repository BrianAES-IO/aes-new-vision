FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Many of our projects start small, with one automation, one app or one security review, and grow from there. We size the work to your budget.

Do you only work with clients in Jamaica?

We're based in St. Catherine, Jamaica, and we work with clients remotely too. Get in touch wherever you are.

After a first conversation, we send you a written proposal with the scope, timeline and cost. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Will AI make decisions for my business?

Only where you want it to. We design automations with human checkpoints, so important approvals and customer decisions stay with your team.
